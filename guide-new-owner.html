CAT GUIDE

New Cat Owner Checklist

A new cat setup doesn't need to be complicated. Focus first on the essentials: food and water supplies, litter setup, a safe resting area, identification and appropriate enrichment.

What to compare

Useful categories include bowls, litter box, litter, carrier, scratcher, toys, grooming supplies and a comfortable resting place. The exact products depend on your cat and household.

Practical tip

Start with essentials, learn your cat's preferences, and add specialized products over time rather than buying everything before you know what your cat uses.
