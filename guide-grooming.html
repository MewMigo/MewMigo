CAT GUIDE

Basic Cat Grooming Supplies

A basic grooming kit can include a brush, nail-care tool and other supplies appropriate for your cat's coat and routine.

What to compare

The right brush depends on coat type. For nail care, use tools designed for cats and follow safe handling guidance. Dental care products should be specifically intended for pets.

Practical tip

If your cat strongly resists grooming or has a coat, skin or nail problem, professional or veterinary guidance may be appropriate.
