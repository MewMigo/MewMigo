CAT GUIDE

Choosing a Litter Box

Litter boxes come in many shapes and sizes, from open pans to covered and automated designs. Your cat's preferences and the available space both matter.

What to compare

Compare box size, entry height, wall height, cleaning access and whether replacement parts are available for automated models. Multiple-cat homes may need more than one box.

Practical tip

Choose a box that you can comfortably clean and that gives your cat adequate room. Introduce changes gradually when possible.
