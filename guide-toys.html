10 Cat Toys for Indoor Play

Indoor cats can benefit from regular opportunities for play and exploration. When comparing toys, consider whether your cat prefers chasing, batting, pouncing, chewing, or solving simple puzzles.

What to compare

Look for toys that match your cat's play style, are appropriate for the cat's size, and can be inspected regularly for damage. Interactive toys should be used according to their instructions and supervised when appropriate.

Practical tip

Start with a small variety rather than buying everything at once. Observe which styles your cat actually uses, then spend more on the categories that work.
