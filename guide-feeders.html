CAT GUIDE

Automatic Cat Feeders

Automatic feeders can help owners manage scheduled meals when their routine makes manual feeding inconvenient.

What to compare

Compare portion controls, schedule flexibility, food capacity, power options, jam resistance and ease of cleaning. For connected models, also consider app requirements and connectivity.

Practical tip

Use the manufacturer's instructions for programming and portion settings. Any automated feeding setup should still be checked regularly rather than treated as completely unattended.
