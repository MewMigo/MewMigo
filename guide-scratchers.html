Choosing a Cat Scratcher

Scratching is a normal cat behavior. A scratcher gives your cat an appropriate surface and can help direct that behavior away from furniture.

What to compare

Compare vertical and horizontal formats, height, stability and scratching material. Some cats prefer sisal while others show more interest in cardboard or fabric surfaces.

Practical tip

Place a scratcher near areas where your cat already scratches or spends time. Offering more than one style can help you learn what your cat prefers.
