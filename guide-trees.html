How to Choose a Cat Tree

A cat tree can combine climbing, resting and scratching in one piece of furniture. The right size depends on your cat, available floor space and where you plan to place it.

What to compare

Compare the footprint, height, base stability, platform sizes, scratching surfaces and washable or replaceable components. A tall structure needs a stable base and should be assembled according to its instructions.

Practical tip

Measure the intended space first. If your cat enjoys looking out windows, placement near a suitable viewing area may make the furniture more useful.
