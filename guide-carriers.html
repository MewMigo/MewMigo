CAT GUIDE

How to Choose a Cat Carrier

A carrier should provide enough room for your cat to sit, turn around and rest comfortably while remaining practical to transport.

What to compare

Compare dimensions, ventilation, door placement, construction, cleaning access and how easily the carrier can be secured during travel. Hard-sided and soft-sided designs have different tradeoffs.

Practical tip

If possible, make the carrier a familiar object before a trip. Follow your veterinarian and transportation provider's requirements when traveling.
